Lightweight Build

Purpose-Driven Digital Accessibility

SwiftReset was envisioned and coded by a 14-year-old student innovator in India. Frustrated by the constant disruptions of a lagging computer while trying to study, he designed this utility to provide equal productivity tools for everyone. We believe that a student's ability to learn should never be limited by the speed of their hardware.
